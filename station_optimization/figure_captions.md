**Figure 3. Regional mean travel under PPO and stage greedy.** Circles and 95% intervals use 2,000 paired bootstrap samples of 15 whole event streams (seeds 101–115); diamonds show the saved fast-evaluator differences. The six scenario–year checks pair Medium trend (w = 1), High trend (w = 1), and High proportional growth (w = 0) with 2035 and 2045. Negative differences favor PPO. The two trend configurations match in 2035; High proportional 2035 uses a different vehicle mix. All three 2045 regional mean contrasts favor PPO. Shared stage action-count limits allow different final fleets because a new station includes an engine. In 2045, PPO/greedy have 30/29 engines and 5/5 quints under Medium trend; 31/28 engines and 5/6 quints under High trend; and 30/29 engines and 6/6 quints under High proportional growth. The contrasts combine location, fleet-size, and fleet-type choices. Some other-call intervals are narrower than their markers.

**Figure 4. Local fire-travel consequences: PPO versus stage greedy.** The 2045 Medium scenario uses historical-trend population allocation (w = 1). Differences compare frozen PPO with stage greedy, using tract means pooled over 15 paired E1 event streams. Travel is first-arriving eligible vehicle travel from en route to arrival. Negative values favor PPO. The symmetric color range is ±120 s, shared with the other scenarios for this comparison. Annotations sum fixed baseline fire-rate weights in faster, slower, and unchanged tracts; differences within 10⁻¹⁰ min count as numerical equality. These categories use point estimates. The policies share stage action-count limits and select different final locations and fleets.
